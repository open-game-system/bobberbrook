# Owner's taste (outranks the critic)

From Jon's global instructions and this game's brief. Any round that breaks a veto or loses a liked
thing is reverted, whatever it scored.

## Vetoes
- No faces on objects (bobbers, rods, boats, houses, the sun, the moon, the tackle box). Fishers and
  fish have natural faces only (fish: natural eyes, no cartoon smiles).
- No emoji anywhere (TV, phones, journal, toasts).
- Nothing covers the focal area of the TV scene (the fishers and the water they fish). HUD stays at the edges.
- No character named "Pip".
- Its own art direction: not Rocket Crew's ink-outline look, not papercraft, not felt.
- No words on the kids' devices (pictures, colour and sound only). Words live on the grown-up's phone.
- Nothing scary for a 3-year-old (dark water monsters, sudden loud stings, snapping jaws).

## Liked (keep)
- The concept art's look (concept/web/01-key-art.jpg, 13-group-pool.jpg): chunky, hand-painted,
  warm, WoW-like fantasy lake. Jon asked for "beautiful setting", "definitely 3D and WoW-like".
- Running around and fishing in different places, progression and variety.
- The painted fish sprites (public/art/fish/*.webp) and the Bobberbrook logo and cover.
