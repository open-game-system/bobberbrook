## 2. Largest gap and top fixes

**The largest gap is fisher placement and the camera at Mistmarsh (Camera row, 4).** For 75 s the TV frames two fishers stacked at the dock foot, and Dad's two catches happen behind Juneau. The round 23 step 2 "no stacking on an end-on dock" fix did not cover this dock and approach.

1. **[rules][scene] Spread the fishing spots at Mistmarsh, and every place, by screen space, not world distance.** When someone arrives, assign each fisher a shore slot so no two bodies overlap in the TV camera's projection (check the projected bounding boxes, require at least 1 body-width gap). Mistmarsh 136–211 s and trail@tod-golden fail today. Make this a lab assertion in every place shot.
2. **[scene] Catch push-in frames the catcher and keeps everyone in frame.** Pick a camera offset that clears the other fishers' silhouettes from the catcher and the held fish, and never crops a fisher at the frame edge (36.8 s crops Juneau, 153–156 and 199–204 s hide Dad's fish). Add a lab check: the fish's bounding box is at most 2% occluded.
3. **[scene] Remove the camera snap and judder.** Critically damp the group camera's target. When the group re-forms or members swap order, blend over at least 0.6 s and never re-target within one frame (121.8 s). Fix the dock jitter at 12–13 s, which looks like the target being recomputed from noisy positions: low-pass the centroid.
4. **[ui] Coach line state.** At arrival in a new place, the coach switches to a "fish here, these are its fish" line. Suppress "Dad found X" until the TV discovery beat actually fires (it showed at 120 s, arrival was at 133.9 s). Never fall back to "Explore!" while the family is at a place it just found (136, 207, 226 s).
5. **[audio] Moonwell catch and hook stings.** Low-pass the sting layers above about 4 kHz, soften the attacks (at least 15 ms), and duck the narrator's consonant bed so the hook and catch sections drop from 25–37 to under 5 harsh onsets per minute (audio-report sections 4–11; spectrogram 30–56 s).
6. **[scene] Golden hour as light, not a tint.** Remove the full-frame magenta grade. Use a low warm key from the west, keep the canopies' purple local colour and the cool teal fill in the shadows, and use rim light on the fishers (moonwell_tod-golden.png, trail_tod-golden.png).
7. **[ui][rules] Make TV causes legible.** Show a short TV cue for each major state change: a fog-trail beacon and a path glow when the family heads out (59 s), a golden swirl that pulses with a ring and a reward icon, and a campfire banner tied to the phone tap (212 s). On the iPad, the reel button should show why it turned orange: a tension ring or a shaking icon (146 s). Replace the grey ghost fish on the iPad catch card (strip-ipad.png, frame 6) with the full-colour fish.
8. **[rig] Fix the check rig and the frozen windows.** 5 of 6 check screens timed out at page.goto, so kid-hat-picker, the phone and iPad controllers and tv-two-fishers went unverified. Find out whether the 13 frozen windows (64–74 s, 210–223 s) are capture stalls or real TV stalls, by correlating them with busy.jsonl. Also find the cause of the four-reel soak p95 of 34.4 ms.

Next after these: re-sculpt the fishers toward 08-characters.jpg (vest, pouches, boots, a real face, painted cloth instead of gloss) [scene]. This is the biggest Art and Chars lever but the most work.
