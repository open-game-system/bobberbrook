## Top 8 fixes (score impact per effort)

1. **[rules] Shore slots per place.** Each fishing spot reserves N separated stand-points along the shore, ≥1.6 m apart, never between dock posts. A fisher who walks to an occupied one snaps to the nearest free one, and a tag-along kid gets their own slot, not the leader's. This fixes Mistmarsh 266–316 s and arrival/trail stacks. (Camera, Catch)
2. **[scene] Occluder fade + dock post cull for the group cam.** Any post, lantern or rail between the camera and a fisher's capsule fades to 20% (Mistmarsh dock lanterns over Dad at 274/290/298 s). Also frame the walking group so no fisher lands under the QR safe zone (255 s; make the QR card a hard keep-out rect in the framing solver). (Camera)
3. **[scene] Re-material the Hollow mushrooms and Mistmarsh cottages with the painted kit.** Mushrooms: hollow.png. Cottages: mistmarsh-dock.png. Use painted gill and stem diffuse, matte, no spec. The cottages get bark/plaster atlas tiles, a roof overhang, beams and door trim like ref-day.jpg. (Art, Env)
4. **[rules] New place = new species.** Mistmarsh's table must not return a Moonwell species until its own list is caught (Stripy Perch at 298 s). Show "new!" on the TV ticket only for first catches. (Progress, Catch)
5. **[audio] Catch sting rework.** Replace the bright pluck cluster with one soft marimba or celesta 3-note rise plus a low water "bloop", low-passed at 4 kHz. Cap at one sting per catch and duck it when two catches land within 6 s. Target <5 harsh/min at the Moonwell hooks and catches (currently 23–36). Add distinct ambience beds: Moonwell falls, trail birds and crickets, Mistmarsh frogs, campfire crackle. (Audio)
6. **[ui] Golden swirl and night cause-and-effect on the TV.** The swirl gets a visible countdown ring on the water and a "missed" fizzle when it times out (172 s). "Night falls" shows the 1–2 night-only fish silhouettes on the TV chip and in the iPad picture (303 s). (Onboard, Progress)
7. **[ui] iPad reel cue + walking state.** The hold dial pulses or shakes with the fish's pull and flashes white the instant the TV splash fires (the release cue lives on the device too). While the family is travelling, hide the cast button's water badge and always show the follow-leader arrow (215 s had none). Clear the coach's "race to the swirl" line once the leader heads elsewhere, and replace "Explore!" with a fishing line once at a pond (303–316 s). (Kid UX, Feel, Phone UI)
8. **[scene] Characters toward the concept.** Give the vests the concept's texture (canvas painted diffuse, pocket flaps, stitching, a strap), break the peg silhouette with a slight torso taper, neck and hip, and differentiate Mom (blue vest, braid, as in 08-characters.jpg) instead of an orange slab. Add a contact shadow blob under each fisher. (Chars, Light)
