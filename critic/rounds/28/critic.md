## Onboarding: what caused each outcome I saw

1. **Cast (8.5 s).** The big teal rod button with a down-into-water badge was pressed while the fisher stood at the water. It's clear from the screen.
2. **Bite (15 s, 80 s).** The bobber close-up splashes and the action button turns into an orange fish. Dad's coach line says "Your bobber dipped: tap now!" It's clear.
3. **Catch (17.4 s, 91.9 s).** The fisher held the button and a fish card burst onto the iPad. *Why* the reel took 2 s at Moonwell but 12 s at Mistmarsh (80→91.9 s) can't be read from the screens. The iPad reel ring flips between green and red/orange about every second (reel burst, 80–92 s) and its arc keeps resetting, so neither a kid nor a grown-up can tell how close the fish is or what red asks for. The coach line ("let go when the fish splashes") is the only explanation, and it is on Dad's phone only.
4. **"Little Teapot" (18 s).** Dad pulls up a teapot. Nothing tells you why it was a teapot and not a fish, or whether that is good or bad.
5. **Star next to a species (17.4, 30.5, 86.6 s).** It reads as "new for the journal" once you see the 1/30 → 2/30 counter tick. That's learnable.
6. **Discovery (about 57 s).** Walking into the new place triggers a "NEW PLACE Mistmarsh" plate on the TV and a star burst on the iPad. It's clear.
7. **Bamboo rods (114.5 s).** The shell bar (+1/+2 after catches) fills, then "Bamboo rods for everyone!" appears. Half-learnable: the shell bar is a thin strip at the top right and nothing links the shells to the fish you just caught.
8. **Campfire (116.9 s).** The cause is Dad's Campfire button, which the TV doesn't show. From the couch, everyone just teleports.

I can explain more than three of these from the screens, but the core verb (the reel) and its result are not legible. That caps the row at 5.

## Single largest actionable gap

**The reel tug-of-war is the core verb, and nobody can read it.** On the iPad it is a button that flickers green and red with an arc that resets (80–92 s). On the TV the fisher stands still with his back to the camera for 9–12 s (82–90 s TV burst). That one gap holds down Feel, Onboarding, Kid UX and Catch at the same time.

## Top 8 fixes (score impact per effort)

1. **[ui] One readable reel meter on the iPad and the TV.** Replace the resetting arc with a monotonic "fish coming in" fill: a fish icon sliding along a line from the bobber to the fisher. Red means "let go now" and pulses with the splash. Mirror it on the TV as a small world-space gauge over the reeling fisher, at the edge of the fisher's box and not over the water focal area. Evidence: 80–92 s iPad burst, 82–90 s TV. Moves Feel, Onboard and Kid UX.
2. **[rules] Cap the kid's reel at about 4–5 s and make "let go" forgiving on Juneau's seat.** The 12 s reel at 80.1→91.9 s and the 9 s reel at 103.4→112.1 s are too long for a 5-year-old, and the result doesn't depend on anything he can see. Moves Feel and Kid UX.
3. **[scene] Reel body language.** Have the fisher lean back, the rod bend toward the fish and the line twitch, with a splash plume at each splash (the "let go" cue). The rig should see a visible pose change at least every 0.5 s during a reel. Evidence: Juneau is static for 12 s in the 82–90 s TV burst. Moves Chars, Feel and Catch.
4. **[scene] Push in on every catch at Mistmarsh, and keep fishers from overlapping.** Dad's Silver Minnow (86.6 s) gets no push-in, and Juneau's hat overlaps Dad's legs. night-catch_push.png leaves the catcher tiny at the left edge: the push target should fill about 30% of frame height. Moves Camera and Catch.
5. **[scene] Remove the foreground occluders the cover check misses.** Take out the dark navy sail/banner wedge at the right of mistmarsh-dock-foot.png and its _push, the brown root wedge at bottom centre of mistmarsh.png, and the corner bokeh discs. Extend the cover check to any object larger than 5% of the frame inside the safe area, not only over fishers. Moves Env and Camera.

7. **[ui] Give a new species a bigger moment than a repeat or junk.** On the TV, show a larger single-species card with a "NEW" glow and a sparkle chord when the star applies, and push the teapot to a small second line. Add a picture-only "junk" cue on the iPad (a teapot with a wink sparkle, no words) and one coach line explaining it. Evidence: 17.5 s, 112 s. Moves Catch and Onboard.
8. **[ui] Coach-line priority: live action beats exploration.** While any fisher is reeling, the coach line must be about that reel ("Juneau's fish is splashing, tell him to let go!"), never "Explore!…" (107 s).
